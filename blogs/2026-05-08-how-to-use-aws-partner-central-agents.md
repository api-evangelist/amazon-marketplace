Amazon Web Services (AWS) recently launched AWS Partner Central agents, a capability partners can use to take advantage of generative AI to advance their business outcomes and streamline the AWS Partner Central experience. AWS Partner Central agents support pipeline insights, opportunity summaries, sales play generation, customer profile creation, and funding recommendations. To learn more, refer to Introducing AWS Partner Central agents. AWS Partner Central agents streamline how you manage opportunities by helping you focus on business outcomes.
