We'll demonstrate how to consolidate your AWS Marketplace license and audit data into an S3 data lake, creating a foundation for advanced analytics. You can connect this centralized repository of data to your preferred business intelligence tools and use it to build custom visualizations and reports that meet your organization's specific needs. This data lake approach provides the flexibility and scalability you need.
